## Recommended shortlist

1. **SubsidieBewijs** — Best fit for collecting invoices, payment proof, installation photos and supporting documents. Broad enough for heat pumps and insulation. Suggested subtitle: **Je documenten op orde voor je subsidieaanvraag.** Avoid presenting the product as an official certificate or an approval service.
2. **WarmteDossier** — Professional and memorable for a heat-pump-first product, with room for insulation and other home-heating improvements. Needs a subtitle to explain the subsidy purpose. Suggested subtitle: **Alles voor je warmtepomp- en isolatiesubsidie bij elkaar.**
3. **MijnSubsidieMap** — Friendly, literal description of a personal document organizer. Suggested subtitle: **Verzamel, controleer en download je subsidiedossier.** The shorter **SubsidieMap.nl** is registered, creating a possible naming-confusion issue.

**DossierGroen** is the strongest broader-brand alternative, but its name alone does not communicate subsidies. **SubsidieOrdner** is very clear but less memorable. **SubsidieBundel** has an ambiguous meaning: a collection of subsidy schemes rather than the applicant's evidence.

## Collision caveats

Focused public searches found no prominent exact-name commercial brand for SubsidieBewijs, WarmteDossier, MijnSubsidieMap, DossierGroen or SubsidieOrdner. This was **not a completed BOIP trademark or trade-name clearance**. An unindexed business may still use a name; domain availability does not establish naming rights.

- **SubsidieBewijs** appears as a generic term in older subsidy legislation. No competing homeowner organizer with that exact name surfaced in the focused search.
- **MijnSubsidieMap** resembles the registered domain **SubsidieMap.nl**; no clearly indexed business behind the shorter name was established.
- **SubsidieBundel** is already used as a publication title by Fiscount and as a generic administrative term. Source: https://www.fiscount.nl/content/uploads/2020/02/Subsidiebundel-2020-1.pdf

Recommendation: offer **SubsidieBewijs.nl** first. Confirm the owner's choice and recheck availability before any purchase or rebranding.
